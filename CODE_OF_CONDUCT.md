# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Setu-TS to be a welcoming, professional place to discuss software design, report defects,
and contribute code. Everyone participating in this project agrees to act in good faith and treat
other participants with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and kind when discussing code, APIs, documentation, and decisions.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification or disagreeing.
- Focus critique on ideas and observable behaviour, never on a person's identity, experience, or
  worth.
- Respect different levels of experience and make room for thoughtful questions.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Knowingly filing spam, fabricated reports, or abusive automated contributions.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in GitHub Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews, project-owned chat or
events, and other spaces where someone represents Setu-TS.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately through the repository's private reporting route described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Prefix a non-security report with `CODE OF CONDUCT:` so maintainers can
route it correctly. Do not discuss a report publicly.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially, seek enough context to make a fair decision, and may
request a correction, remove content, limit participation, or ban a participant. Retaliation against
a reporter or someone involved in an investigation is not permitted.
